Datastream has these constraints:
Payload size: 23 bytes
total datagram size: 32 bytes (including header and CRC)
FEC coding adds 8 bytes of redundancy, with a total of 40 bytes after coding.
FEC can correct up to 8 byte errors in the coded message.

Convolutional coding:
adds 1 byte tail, and the output is 2 bits per input bit, resulting in 82 bytes after convolutional coding.

Modulation:
BPSK modulation maps each bit to one symbol, resulting in 656 symbols after modulation.
QPSK modulation maps 2 bits to one symbol, resulting in 328 symbols after modulation.

Pad front and back with Gold Sequence, each of length 31
BPSK: 656 + 31 + 31 = 718 symbols
QPSK: 328 + 31 + 31 = 390

Upsampling factor: 8
BPSK: 718 * 8 = 5744 samples
QPSK: 390 * 8 = 3120 samples

Pad with filter length - 1 = 40 zeros at the end for filtering
BPSK: 5744 + 2*40 = 5824 samples
QPSK: 3120 + 2*40 = 3200 samples

Add Guard Interval of 32 samples front and back
BPSK: 5824 + 32 + 32 = 5888 samples
QPSK: 3200 + 32 + 32 = 3264 samples
